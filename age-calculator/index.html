What exact age means

Your age is the time from your date of birth to a chosen date, written as whole years, then whole months, then days. It is not the same as the current year minus your birth year, which is wrong by one for anyone whose birthday has not yet come round this year.

years  = whole years from the birth date to the chosen date
months = whole months since the last birthday
days   = days since that month anniversary

How to use the calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Leave Age on as today, or change it to any later date, such as the day an age limit applies.
  3. Read the age, the next birthday and the weekday you were born. The table under the result gives total months, weeks and days.

Worked example: born 15 May 1990, measured on 4 November 2026. The last birthday was 15 May 2026, which makes 36 years. From 15 May to 15 October is 5 months, and from 15 October to 4 November is 20 days. The age is 36 years, 5 months, 20 days, or 13,322 days in total.

Someone born on 4 November 2008 is exactly 18 on the reference day, so the months and days are zero. Born one day later, on 5 November 2008, they are still 17 and their birthday is a day away.

Why the same number of years is not always the same number of days

A leap year has 366 days, so the number of leap days inside a span changes the total. Someone born on 1 January 2000 and someone born on 1 January 2001 both reach their 18th birthday after 18 years, but not after the same number of days.

Leap years and 29 February

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except that a century year must be divisible by 400. The extra day keeps the calendar in step with a solar year of about 365.2422 days (NIST, see Sources).

A person born on 29 February has no birthday in a common year. This calculator uses 28 February in those years, so for someone born on 29 February 2004 the next birthday after 16 November 2026 is 28 February 2027, and after 16 November 2027 it is 29 February 2028. Another reasonable rule is 1 March. Which one applies to a legal age limit depends on the country, so for a legal purpose check the rule yourself.

Month ends

Months have 28 to 31 days, so “one month after the 31st” needs a rule. The calculator adds whole months to the birth date and moves back to the last day of the month when the day does not exist. That is why someone born on 31 January 2000 is 26 years, 1 months, 0 days old on 28 February 2026 but 26 years, 1 months, 1 days old on 1 March. The same rule is used by the date calculator, and the guide to counting days between dates explains the reasoning.

What this calculator leaves out

It works on calendar dates only. It does not use the time of birth or time zones, so it cannot tell you the hour you turn a year older. It uses the Gregorian calendar throughout, so it is not meant for dates in other calendar systems. It does not decide legal ages: the age of majority, school entry and retirement rules are set by each country, and so are the rules for 29 February. For the gap between two dates that are not a birth date, use the days between dates calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated in years, months and days?

Whole calendar years are counted from the date of birth, then whole months, then the remaining days. Someone born on 15 May 1990 is, on 4 November 2026, 36 years, 5 months and 20 days old.

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Subtract the birth year from the current year. If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract one more. Then count the whole months since your last birthday, and the days since the month anniversary. The calculator does all three steps at once.

How many days old am I?

The Total days row counts every calendar day from your birth date to the date measured, leap days included. Someone born on 15 May 1990 is 13,322 days old on 4 November 2026.

Does the day of birth count?

The age is measured from the birth date to the chosen date, so the birth date itself is day zero. You turn a year older on your birthday, not the day after.

How is a 29 February birthday handled?

In years that are not leap years, this calculator treats 28 February as the anniversary. Born on 29 February 2004, you are 22 years, 0 months, 0 days old on 28 February 2026 and one day older on 1 March. Some places legally use 1 March instead, so for a legal purpose check the rule that applies to you.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the Age on date. It starts as today, but you can set any date after the date of birth, such as the first day of a school year or the day an age limit applies.

How do I find the age difference between two people?

Enter the older person's birth date as the date of birth and the younger person's birth date in Age on. The result is the gap between them in years, months and days.

What weekday was I born on?

The Born on a tile shows it. 15 May 1990, for example, was a Tuesday.

Why do two age calculators sometimes disagree by a day?

Month ends. Whole months are added to the birth date, and if the day does not exist in the target month it is moved back to the last day. Born on 31 January 2000, you are 26 years, 1 months, 0 days old on 28 February 2026 and 26 years, 1 months, 1 days old on 1 March. Another tool may count the days differently, so both answers can be defensible.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
