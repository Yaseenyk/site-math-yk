The formulas

mean   = sum / count
median = the middle value (or the mean of the middle two)
mode   = the most frequent value
range  = maximum - minimum

Worked example

For 4, 8, 8, 10, 15: the sum is 45 and the count is 5, so the mean is 9. In order, the middle value is 8, so the median is 8. The value 8 appears twice, so the mode is 8. The range is 11.

Which average to quote

  • Mean uses every value and is the right choice for evenly spread data such as test scores, but a single outlier pulls it.
  • Median is the middle value and ignores how extreme the ends are, so it describes a typical case for skewed figures such as salaries or house prices. With an even count it is the mean of the middle two: for 3, 5, 7, 10 that is (5 + 7) / 2 = 6.
  • Mode is the most common value, useful for categories such as shoe sizes. A list can have no mode, or several.

A weighted average is different

When values do not count equally, the plain mean is wrong. A course is graded 30% on an exam where you scored 80 and 70% on coursework where you scored 90. The weighted average is 0.30 x 80 + 0.70 x 90 = 24 + 63 = 87, not the plain mean of 85. Multiply each value by its weight, add the results, and divide by the total weight (here 1). This calculator gives the plain mean; for the spread of the values, use the standard deviation calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the average of a set of numbers?

Add the numbers and divide by how many there are. For 4, 8, 8, 10 and 15 the sum is 45 and there are 5 numbers, so the average is 9.

What is the difference between mean, median and mode?

The mean is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value when the numbers are in order. The mode is the value that appears most often.

When is the median better than the mean?

When a few extreme values would pull the mean away from a typical value. For 1, 2, 3, 4 and 100 the mean is 22, but the median is 3.

What if two values tie for the mode?

Then there are two modes, and the calculator lists both. If every value appears once, there is no mode.

What is the range?

The largest value minus the smallest. For 4, 8, 8, 10 and 15, the range is 15 - 4 = 11.

How do I find the median of an even number of values?

Take the mean of the two middle values. For 3, 5, 7 and 10 the middle two are 5 and 7, so the median is 6 (the mean is 6.25).

How do I calculate a weighted average?

Multiply each value by its weight, add the results and divide by the total weight. A score of 80 worth 30% and 90 worth 70% gives 0.3 x 80 + 0.7 x 90 = 87. This calculator treats every value equally.

Can I average percentages?

Only if each percentage is of the same total. Otherwise, add up the underlying parts and totals and take the percentage of those.
