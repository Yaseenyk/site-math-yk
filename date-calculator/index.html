When people use it

  • Payment terms: an invoice dated 1 December 2026 with net 45 terms is due on 15 January 2027, a Friday.
  • Deadlines and notice periods: 90 days from 14 November 2026 is 12 February 2027.
  • Looking back: 60 days before 1 March 2026 is 31 December 2025.
  • Leap years: 31 January 2028 plus 1 month is 29 February 2028.

Why months need care

A month is not a fixed number of days, so adding a month moves to the same day number in the next month. When that day does not exist, the result is the last day of the month. To measure the gap between two dates instead, use the days between dates calculator.

Weeks, days and chained months

Adding weeks is adding 7 days each: 4 weeks after 4 November 2026 is 28 days later, 2 December 2026. Months are different because the end-of-month rule is applied once, to the final result. Adding 2 months to 31 January 2026 gives 31 March 2026, the same day number. Adding 1 month twice, one step at a time, gives 28 February and then 28 March, because the first step already lost the 31st. So when you need a date a number of months ahead, add all the months in one go from the original date.

The calculator works on calendar dates, not timestamps, so a daylight-saving change never moves the answer by a day. It does not know public holidays or weekends: if a deadline falls on one, check the rule in the contract or law that sets it, since some roll to the next working day and some do not. The guide to counting days between dates covers inclusive counting and working days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add days to a date?

Choose the start date, leave the operation on Add, enter the number of days, and read the result. 30 days after 4 November 2026 is 4 December 2026, a Friday.

How do I find the date 90 days from today?

Leave the start date as today and enter 90 in Days. Starting from 1 January 2026, 90 days later is 1 April 2026.

What happens when I add a month to the 31st?

If the target month is shorter, the date moves to its last day. 31 January 2026 plus 1 month is 28 February 2026, and in a leap year it is 29 February.

In what order are years, months and days applied?

Years and months are added first, as whole calendar months, and then the days. Doing the months first matters because month lengths vary.

How do I go back in time?

Change the operation to Subtract. For example, subtracting 6 months from 15 March 2026 gives 15 September 2025.

What is the date 90 days from 14 November 2026?

12 February 2027, a Friday. Add 90 days to the start date and the calculator also shows the weekday.

What is 29 February plus one year?

The result is 28 February of the next year when that year is not a leap year. 29 February 2028 plus 1 year is 28 February 2029.

Does this count working days?

No, it adds calendar days. To count working days between two dates, use the days between dates calculator.
