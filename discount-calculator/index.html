The formulas

sale price     = original x (1 - discount / 100)
you save       = original - sale price
percent off    = (original - sale) / original x 100

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a sale price?

Multiply the original price by 1 minus the discount as a decimal. An $80 item at 25% off costs 80 x 0.75 = $60, so you save $20.

How do I find the percent off from two prices?

Subtract the sale price from the original, divide by the original, and multiply by 100. From $120 down to $90 is (120 - 90) / 120 x 100 = 25% off.

What do two discounts in a row add up to?

They multiply, they do not add. 20% off and then a further 10% off leaves 0.80 x 0.90 = 72% of the price, which is 28% off in total, not 30%. Use the extra discount box to see it.

How do I work out the original price from the sale price?

Divide the sale price by 1 minus the discount. If an item costs $60 after 25% off, the original price was 60 / 0.75 = $80.

Does it include sales tax?

No. The calculator works on the price you enter. Sales tax is usually added after the discount, so multiply the sale price by 1 plus your tax rate.
