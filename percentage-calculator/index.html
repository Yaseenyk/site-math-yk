A quick way to do it in your head

Ten percent of a number is the number with the decimal point moved one place left: 10% of 80 is 8. Five percent is half of that, 4. So 15% of 80 is 8 + 4 = 12. One percent is the decimal point moved two places.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate X% of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. 15% of 80 is 15 / 100 x 80 = 12.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. 12 is 12 / 80 x 100 = 15% of 80.

How do I find the whole number when I know a part and the percentage?

Divide the part by the percentage as a decimal. If 12 is 15% of a number, the number is 12 / 0.15 = 80.

How do I calculate a percentage of a percentage?

Multiply them as decimals. 20% of 50% is 0.20 x 0.50 = 0.10, which is 10%. Use the first calculator and enter 20 as the percentage and 50 as the number.

How do I turn a percentage into a decimal?

Divide by 100, which moves the decimal point two places left. 15% is 0.15, and 7.5% is 0.075.
