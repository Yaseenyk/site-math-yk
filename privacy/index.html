Privacy Policy

This policy describes what StreamerOS Math Calculators (https://math.streamerosai.com) does with information when you use it. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What the tools do with your input

The calculators work entirely in your browser. The numbers and dates you type, including any date of birth, are not transmitted to our servers, logged, or stored. Closing the page clears them.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. The analytics and advertising services listed below may set cookies or read similar identifiers. The tools themselves do not need a cookie to work. The only thing the site stores for its own use is your light or dark theme choice, kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the tools keep working either way.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful. It collects page views, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived, using cookies and similar identifiers. It does not receive the text you enter into the tools. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. We do not sell personal information.

Usage recording

This site uses Microsoft Clarity to see how pages are used, through heatmaps and anonymised session recordings (mouse movement, clicks and scrolling). Clarity is configured to mask the text on the page, so what you type or load into a tool is not recorded. Clarity sets its own cookies (_clck, _clsk) and Microsoft cookies on clarity.ms and bing.com (such as MUID); see Microsoft's privacy statement.

First-party visit counter

The site also runs a small counter of its own, Pulse, a script served from our own Cloudflare Worker (pulse.yaseenyk.workers.dev), to tell real visits from automated ones. It records the page address (without the query string), the referring site and campaign tags, country, device type, browser and system name, screen size, language, the name of your network provider, scroll depth, time on the page, page speed, which links and buttons were clicked, and a human-or-bot signal. It sets no cookies, never reads what you type or the values in input fields, and stores no IP address or browser string: the address is used in memory only, to limit abuse and to make a visitor code that changes every day (a one-way hash with a random salt that is deleted the next day), so visits cannot be linked across days. Records are deleted after 90 days. It switches itself off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and on localhost. The data is used only to understand how the site is used.

Advertising

This site does not show ads at the moment. We intend to use Google AdSense after approval. Once ads run, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, and learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), personalised ads will be shown only after you consent. Ads will never change a tool's result, and this policy will be updated before ads go live if anything above changes.

Your choices

You can use the site with cookies blocked, opt out of Google Analytics with the add-on above, turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to switch off the visit counter, and ask a privacy question through the contact page. We do not collect accounts, names or email addresses through the tools. If you write to us, we use your address only to reply.

Children

The tools are general-purpose and not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact page. The rules for using the site are in the terms of use.
