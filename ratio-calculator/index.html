What a ratio is

A ratio compares two quantities by how many times one fits into the other. 6 : 4 says that for every 6 of the first there are 4 of the second. Dividing both terms by the same number gives an equivalent ratio, and the simplest form uses the smallest whole numbers: 6 : 4 is 3 : 2.

simplify:      divide A and B by gcd(A, B)
solve:         A : B = C : X  ->  X = B x C / A
share a total: part = total x A / (A + B)

How to use the calculator

  1. To simplify, type the two terms into A and B. The simplest form, the share of each part and the 1 : n form appear under them.
  2. To find a missing value, keep A and B and type C. The answer is X in A : B = C : X, which is the same ratio at a different scale.
  3. To share an amount in the ratio A : B, type the total in the last box.

Worked example: a recipe uses 2 cups of rice for 3 servings. For 10 cups, X = 3 x 10 / 2 = 15 servings.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Swapping the order. 3 : 2 and 2 : 3 are different ratios. Keep the same order on both sides of the equals sign.
  • Treating the parts as fractions of each other. In 3 : 2, the first part is 3/5 of the whole, not 3/2 of it.
  • Mixing units. Convert both quantities to the same unit before you compare them.

What this calculator leaves out

It works with two terms. Ratios with three or more terms, such as 2 : 3 : 5, are not supported, but you can compare them two terms at a time. It does not convert units for you, and it does not check that a proportion is reasonable for your problem: ratios of unlike things, such as price per item, are rates and need their units kept. To express a part as a percent of the whole, use the percentage calculator; to compare how a value changed, use the percent change calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor. For 6:4 the divisor is 2, so the ratio simplifies to 3:2.

How do I simplify a ratio with decimals?

Multiply both numbers by the same power of 10 to make them whole, then simplify. 1.5:2 becomes 15:20, which simplifies to 3 : 4.

How do I find the missing number in a ratio?

Use cross-multiplication. For A:B = C:X, X = B x C / A. If 2:3 = 10:X, then X = 3 x 10 / 2 = 15.

How do I divide a number in a given ratio?

Add the parts of the ratio, divide the total by that sum, and multiply by each part. To split 500 in the ratio 3:2, the sum is 5, so each part is 100 and the shares are 300 and 200. The third box in the calculator does this.

How do I scale a ratio for a screen or image?

For a 16:9 screen that is 1920 wide, the height is 9 x 1920 / 16 = 1,080. Keep the ratio and the picture is not stretched.

Is a ratio the same as a fraction?

They are related but not identical. The ratio 3:2 compares two parts to each other. As fractions of the whole, the parts are 3/5 and 2/5, which is 60% and 40%.

How do I turn a ratio into a percentage?

Divide each part by the sum of the parts and multiply by 100. For 1:3 the sum is 4, so the parts are 25% and 75%. The line under the simplified ratio shows this as you type.

What is the difference between 2:3 and 3:2?

Order matters. 2:3 means the first quantity is two thirds of the second; 3:2 means it is one and a half times the second. Keep the same order on both sides when you solve A:B = C:X.

Can I use decimals or unequal units in a ratio?

Decimals are fine. Units must match on both sides, though: compare metres with metres, not metres with centimetres. Convert one side first, then enter the numbers.

What does the calculator do with a zero?

A ratio with a zero part, such as 0:5, simplifies to 0:1, and A : B = C : X cannot be solved when A is 0, because it would divide by zero. A:B with both parts 0 has no ratio.
